Questions we get often

How fast can you arrange a truck?

For common routes, usually within 24 hours of confirming the booking.

How is the freight rate decided?

By distance, truck type, load weight and route conditions. The calculator above gives an indicative figure, and we confirm the final rate before dispatch.

Can I send a part load?

Yes. Share the weight and size of your goods and we will check space on a truck going your way.

Do you cover all of India?

We move freight on major routes across the country. Ask us about your specific lane.

How do I get updates on my shipment?

Call or message us on WhatsApp any time and we will share the truck's current location.
